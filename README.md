# Guardian Institute of Safety Studies (GISS)

🎓 Advanced Safety Training Academy

## 🏆 About Us
Guardian Institute of Safety Studies (GISS) is an MSME Registered and ISO 9001:2015 Certified online academy dedicated to delivering advanced safety training programs.

We specialize in developing highly skilled safety professionals with advanced technical knowledge, practical skills, and international safety standards.

---

## 📚 Our Advanced Training Programs

### 🔥 Core Advanced Safety Training
- Advanced Fire Safety & Risk Assessment
- Construction Safety Management
- Industrial Safety Engineering
- Oil & Gas Safety Training
- Electrical Safety & LOTO (Lockout/Tagout)
- Work at Height Safety
- Confined Space Entry Training
- Permit to Work (PTW) System

---

### ⚙️ Specialized & Modern Safety Modules
- HAZOP (Hazard and Operability Study)
- HIRA (Hazard Identification & Risk Assessment)
- Behaviour Based Safety (BBS)
- Process Safety Management (PSM)
- Incident Investigation & Root Cause Analysis (RCA)
- Safety Audit & Inspection Techniques
- Emergency Response & Disaster Management
- Environmental Health & Safety (EHS)

---

### 🌍 Industry-Focused & International Modules
- OSHA-Based Safety Awareness
- ISO Safety Management Systems Overview
- Workplace Safety Compliance Training
- Safety Officer Career Development Program
- Advanced Risk Management Techniques
- Industrial Hazard Control Systems
